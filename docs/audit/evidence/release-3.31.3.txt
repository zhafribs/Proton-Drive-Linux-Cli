## Changes

- A modal with a spinner and explanation is shown above every page while an
  update downloads and verifies. It stays until the app restarts.
- Starting an update also queues a toast.
- `--diagnose` writes through a sink that ignores `EPIPE`, so `| head` no
  longer aborts with "failed printing to stdout".
