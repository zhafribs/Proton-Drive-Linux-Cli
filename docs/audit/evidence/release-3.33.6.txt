# Release evidence — v0.9.0-gui3.33.6

Tag: v0.9.0-gui3.33.6
Purpose: add a restrained, semantic colour layer to the Files list without
turning the app into a dashboard.

## Design council

Three seats reviewed the proposal before implementation:

- **UI designer** — PASS: glyph-only colour, separate `FILE_*` namespace,
  folders keep the accent, selection keeps its white glyph. Image hue must
  separate from folder violet by lightness as well as hue.
- **Accessibility / 508** — HOLD until magenta, teal and amber left the status
  palette and moved to tested shades; PASS once every pair was gated at 3:1 on
  both `PAGE` and `SURFACE`. Also flagged the light hover row, which blended
  the fill to mid grey.
- **Finish gate** — PASS: no chips, tints, rows or filename colour; status
  hues stay reserved for status.

## Changes

- File glyphs are tinted by type: violet folders, blue documents, dusty rose
  images, teal media, olive archives, muted everything else.
- `FILE_*` tokens live in their own namespace in `gui/src/theme.rs`; no chrome,
  card, pill or background uses them.
- Light and dark are separate pairs, each contrast-tested at 3:1 on `PAGE` and
  `SURFACE`; `theme::tests` now covers all four surfaces per pair.
- Light-theme hover rows use `ACCENT_HOVER` directly instead of a 45 %
  darkened blend that had dropped text and icons below AA on mouse-over.
- `docs/design/CLARITY.md` §3.3 records the glyph-only rule and the namespace
  boundary.
- The English and Malay screenshot matrices were regenerated.
