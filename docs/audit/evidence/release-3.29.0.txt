## UI audit

`scripts/ui-check.sh` (6 tests):

  every_page_renders_at_the_minimum_window
  keyboard_only_reaches_settings_and_activates_it
  status_variants_render_without_missing_names
  dialogs_render_without_missing_names
  screenshot_matrix_covers_pages_and_scales
  kittest_renders_a_frame

- 7 pages (Home, My files, Photos, Activity, Settings, About, Recovery) at
  720×400.
- 4 pages at 50% (1440×800 logical) and 200% (720×400 logical on a 2x surface).
- 14 dialogs and 7 status variants; AccessKit fails any input widget without a
  screen-reader name.
- Keyboard: Tab visits at least 5 controls, reaches the Settings nav row and
  Enter switches to Settings with no pointer events.
- 15 screenshots in `docs/audit/m8/`; CI artifact `ui-audit-screenshots`
  (run 43248371534).

Two accessibility defects found and fixed: the Activity filter combo box had
no accessible name, and the rename/new-folder text field was labelled only by
a sibling label.

## Packaging

- aarch64 AppImage is an AArch64 ELF; AppRun, GUI, engine and CLI all report
  GLIBC_2.28. Both architectures are signed by the same manifest.
- `.deb` files install the tested AppImage under
  `/usr/lib/proton-drive/`, a `/usr/bin/proton-drive` launcher, the desktop
  entry, metainfo and icons; control metadata and installed payload were
  checked for both architectures.
