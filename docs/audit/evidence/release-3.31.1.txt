## M10 deliverables

- **First-run wizard:** welcome → sign in → folder, shown once, skippable,
  rendered by the UI audit. Flatpak shows the exact `flatpak run` sign-in
  command instead of trying to open a host terminal.
- **Flatpak:** manifest, wrapper, desktop entry, icons, metainfo and sandbox
  permissions; release asset and CI install/smoke check. Updates and host
  menu/autostart toggles are explicitly Flatpak-aware.
- **Bahasa Melayu (beta):** language preference and first-run choice;
  translated setup, sidebar, sync status and Home. Other pages stay English
  and the UI says so; see `docs/LOCALIZATION.md`.
- **3.31.1 update fix:** the release-download allowlist now trusts exactly
  `security/key-revocations.json` and `.sig` on `raw.githubusercontent.com`.
  An ignored live-network test fetched the feed, verified the successor
  signature, and passed.
