# Licence and dependency review

**Program:** Full product audit, G4  
**Date:** 2026-11-08

## Project licence

The repository and the Rust GUI/engine are **MIT** (`LICENSE`). The vendored
`vendor/ksni` crate is **Unlicense** with a documented local patch
(`vendor/ksni/PATCH.md`). The official Proton Drive CLI is redistributed
verbatim under **MIT**, copyright Proton AG; its notice ships in every
AppImage.

## Dependency inventory

`cargo metadata` reports no unknown licences and no GPL/AGPL-only dependency.
The only GNU-family strings are dual-licence alternatives we do not select:

| Crate | Offered terms | Selected |
|---|---|---|
| `r-efi` | MIT OR Apache-2.0 OR LGPL-2.1-or-later | MIT/Apache-2.0 |
| `self_cell` | Apache-2.0 OR GPL-2.0-only | Apache-2.0 |
| `ring` | ISC-style | ISC-style |
| one MPL-2.0 dependency | MPL-2.0 | MPL-2.0, unmodified, notice included |

The aggregated text `src/licenses/proton-drive-gui-THIRD-PARTY.txt` is
regenerated by `scripts/gen-licenses.sh` from `Cargo.lock`; CI fails when it
is stale. `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` covers the Proton CLI binary, the app icon,
the vendored crate and the AppImage contents.

## Vulnerability and maintenance scan

`cargo audit` (RustSec database, 2026-11-08):

- **0 vulnerabilities** after updating `rustls-webpki` 0.103.15 → 0.103.16
  (RUSTSEC-2026-0382) and `webpki-roots` 1.0.9 → 1.0.10
  (RUSTSEC-2026-0381).
- 3 unmaintained warnings: `zbus_names`, `zvariant`, `zvariant_derive` (the
  zbus/accesskit/atspi stack that provides the tray and accessibility bridge).
  They have no known vulnerability; replacing them depends on an upstream
  accesskit/ksni update.
- `cargo-machete` reports one unused optional dependency in the vendored
  `ksni` crate (`futures-lite`, only enabled by the unused `async-io` feature).
  Remove it in G5 or record it as an upstream-feature shim.
