# GStack Specialist Personas

Employs specialized persona roles during development and maintenance.

## Personas
- **QA / Tester**: Focuses on edge-case coverage, regression testing, and assertion checks.
- **Debugger**: Performs systematic root-cause diagnosis for subtle failures or lock issues.
- **Architect**: Ensures structural integrity, module isolation, and clean APIs.
